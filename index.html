What it can do

One website for ECG rhythm learning.

The site combines an ECG strip, a synchronized heart conduction model, and a rhythm selector so you can connect the waveform to what is happening inside the heart.

📤

Analyze uploaded ECGs

Upload a clean rhythm-strip image. The browser estimates rate, regularity, QRS width, and maps the strip to the closest supported rhythm.

📚

Study rhythm patterns

Switch between sinus rhythm, AF, flutter, SA blocks, AV blocks, bundle branch blocks, and LVH to compare ECG morphology.

🫀

Link ECG to conduction

Watch the SA node, internodal pathways, AV node, Bundle of His, bundle branches, and Purkinje system activate with the strip.

🧠

Quiz yourself

Generate ECG cases with symptoms, duration, measurements, leads, and either MCQ or typed-answer mode.

Study rhythms

Choose a rhythm and watch the mechanism.

Use the visualizer to compare how rhythm disorders alter atrial activation, AV conduction, ventricular depolarization, and the ECG waveform.

Atrial fibrillationChaotic atrial activity, absent organized P waves, irregular ventricular response.
Atrial flutterMacro-reentry atrial activity with sawtooth flutter waves.
AV blocksPR prolongation, dropped QRS complexes, or complete AV dissociation.
Bundle branch blocksDelayed ventricular activation with widened QRS morphology.
